## Abstract
Hospital emergency departments handle a continuous and unpredictable influx of patients with varying degrees of severity. Manual triage processes are often time-consuming and prone to inconsistency, which can delay the identification of critical cases, increase patient waiting times, and place unnecessary strain on emergency staff. This project proposes an AI-powered patient care platform focused on strengthening emergency management through accurate, real-time triage prioritization.
The platform centers on the patient journey through the emergency pathway, covering patient registration, intake, vitals and symptom capture, triage prioritization, and case tracking through to disposition. Role-based dashboards give doctors, nurses and administrators access to a live, prioritized patient queue along with the underlying clinical details relevant to their responsibilities.
Artificial intelligence is integrated directly into the emergency workflow to support faster and more consistent triage decisions. A triage scoring model, built on established clinical severity indicators and refined using machine learning, evaluates patient vitals and reported symptoms to assign a priority level. The queue automatically re-orders as new patients arrive or conditions change, while staff retain the ability to review and override AI-assigned scores. These overrides are logged and used to continually refine the scoring model, allowing the system to adapt over time.
By combining structured patient management with AI-assisted triage, the project aims to reduce time-to-treatment for critical patients, improve consistency in prioritization decisions, ease the cognitive load on emergency staff during high-volume periods and establish a foundation for a scalable, adaptive approach to emergency patient care.
